David Adeleke (Student) 2 hours ago

In step 3 of the derivation, why do we apply the integrating factor to both sides before evaluating the integral on the right?

Instructor Response (Engr. Ajibade Opeyemi Phillip): Great observation, David! Multiplying both sides by the integrating factor I(x) collapses the entire left-hand side into the exact derivative of a product: d/dx [y · I(x)]. That is what allows us to integrate both sides in a single clean step!
